# Pass: Style Sheet

## Purpose

Keep one written record of the decisions this document has made, so every later pass applies the same decisions instead of making them again.

Opened at step 3 (intent extraction), read by every later pass, and added to as new decisions come up. For long-form work it sits beside the global brief and carries its locked terminology.

## The writer this pass makes

A copy editor who writes each decision down the moment it is made. They know that a term, a name, a number, or a register chosen once and not recorded gets chosen differently by the next pass. Before touching a sentence, they read the sheet.

## What the sheet holds (`00-style-sheet.md`)

One row per decision: **Area | Decision | Decided by | Why**.

- **Reader and register** — who reads, how formal, the address form, how a wish or position is phrased.
- **Terms** — titles, product and program names, and domain terms: the form, the language, and whether it was given by the user or looked up.
- **Numbers** — digits or words, how many are kept, which qualifiers always stay (a level, a date, a scope).
- **Names** — people, institutions, programs, in the form the user gave.
- **Spelling and typography** — diacritics, quotation marks, hyphenation, capitals, abbreviations.
- **Open questions** — decisions that belong to the user, with the options.

"Decided by" is one of **user** (stated or supplied), **source** (a dictionary or guide that was opened), or **writer** (an inference).

## How the passes use it

1. **Open it** at step 3 from what the user gave, the reader brief, and the research note if they exist. Mark each row by who decided it.
2. **Read it before editing.** Every later pass starts from the sheet.
3. **Add to it.** When a pass settles a new choice (a spelling, a term, a number rule), it adds a row before moving on.
4. **Change it openly.** A row decided by the **user** stays as given; if it looks wrong, flag it for the user in Open questions. A row decided by the **writer** may change when the pass records the new decision and the reason.
5. **Flag a clash.** When a sentence or word disagrees with a row, bring the sentence in line with the sheet, or record the change under step 4.

## Output

`00-style-sheet.md` in the scratch folder, kept current through the run. In memory mode, hold it as a short table in context and show it under the result.
